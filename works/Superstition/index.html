Superstition

2025 Digital Print Giclée Paper Limited Edition

About the Work

"Superstition" is a mysterious piece that explores the deep bond between humans and dogs through the lens of photographic artistry. This work showcases my signature use of rich blacks and subtle contrasts to highlight the emotional depth and spiritual connection that exists between species.

Created using photography with digital manipulation, this piece captures the lasting bond we have with dogs while thinking about the prejudices we have in society. The work is printed on high-quality giclée paper to ensure the finest reproduction of the original digital artwork.
